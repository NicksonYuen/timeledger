TIME LEDGER - put it online (free, about 10 minutes, once)

1. Go to github.com and create a free account.
2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it: timeledger. Choose Public. Click Create.
3. On the new page click "uploading an existing file". Drag in ALL files from this folder
   (not the folder itself). Click Commit changes.
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Branch choose "main" and "/ (root)". Click Save.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/timeledger/

ON YOUR IPHONE
6. Open that address in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
   Always open it from the home screen icon. That keeps your data safe and lets it work offline.
7. Reminders: open the Reminders app > new reminder "Log my time" > (i) > Date and Time 1:00 PM
   > Repeat: Daily. Add a second one at 10:00 PM. When it pops up, tap the Time Ledger icon.
   (Don't use a Shortcut that opens the web address: it opens Safari, which can't see your data.)

YOUR DATA
- It stays on your phone only. Nobody else can see it, including anyone who opens the web address.
- Settings > Back up once a month. Keep the file in iCloud Drive or email it to yourself.
- New phone: install the app the same way, then Settings > Restore.
